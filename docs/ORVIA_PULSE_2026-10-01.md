# ORVIA PULSE

## Purpose
ORVIA PULSE is the market-sensing, social-content and campaign-learning layer inside Command.

Its loop is:

LISTEN -> LEARN -> CREATE -> APPROVE -> PUBLISH -> MEASURE -> ADAPT

## What PULSE is for
- monitor authorised/public market sources;
- capture competitor, regulatory, customer and sector signals;
- ingest ORVIA social and campaign performance;
- identify recurring questions and emerging themes;
- prepare evidence-linked content ideas and campaigns;
- require human approval before publication unless a future explicit policy permits a lower-risk exception;
- measure beyond vanity metrics by connecting campaigns to enquiries, qualified leads, proposals, sales and revenue;
- preserve learning so future campaigns improve.

## Evidence discipline
PULSE must not collapse observation and interpretation.

1. MARKET SIGNAL = what a source actually showed.
2. ORVIA PERFORMANCE = what an ORVIA campaign actually achieved.
3. ORVIA INTERPRETATION = what IRIS/Insight thinks those facts may mean.

Interpretations should carry confidence, alternatives and a recommended test.

## Initial source families
These are integration targets, not claims of being live:
- ORVIA website analytics and form attribution;
- LinkedIn / Meta / Instagram / X / YouTube / TikTok where official APIs and account permissions permit;
- email campaign performance;
- ORVIA Voice enquiry themes;
- CRM outcomes;
- authorised RSS/news feeds;
- regulatory and government update pages;
- competitor public websites;
- search / SEO signals;
- public community discussion where collection is lawful, proportionate and permitted by platform terms.

## Open-source evaluation
Useful patterns identified for separate licence and security review:
- Postiz-style scheduling/publishing/analytics;
- Satori-style generated branded graphics;
- Remotion-style reusable short-form video;
- changedetection.io-style public webpage change monitoring;
- RSSHub-style feed aggregation where terms permit;
- BERTopic-style topic clustering.

Do not import or rebrand third-party code until licence obligations, update risk, data flows and security have been reviewed.

## Human control
PULSE may recommend, draft and queue. Publication remains human-approved by default.

Consequential or sensitive messaging must always remain human-reviewed.

## Relationship to ORVIA
IRIS = conductor
PULSE = market/social sensing and learning
HERMES = communications
CRM = relationships and commercial outcomes
HIVE = evidence/provenance
Insight = interpretation
VITA/VERA = challenge/verification where required

PULSE primarily spans O — Observe, I — Interpret and A — Act.
