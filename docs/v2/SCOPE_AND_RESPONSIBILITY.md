# ORVIA V2 Scope and Responsibility Boundary

## ORVIA is

A connected evidence, accountability and assurance operating system that captures information, preserves source and provenance, coordinates accountable work, makes omissions and failures visible, supports authorised human decisions, records verification, and checks whether action worked.

## ORVIA is not

- an emergency service;
- a substitute for 999, 111, safeguarding referral routes, police, local authority or regulator notification;
- a clinical decision-maker;
- a credibility assessor;
- a disciplinary tribunal;
- an autonomous safeguarding decision system;
- a replacement for a tenant's accountable professionals.

## Customer responsibility

The tenant remains responsible for:
- staffing its accountable roles and escalation chains;
- emergency, statutory, safeguarding and professional decisions;
- the legality and proportionality of actions it chooses;
- deciding who may access records;
- approving safety-relevant workflow configuration;
- acting when ORVIA indicates an overdue, blocked or failed process.

## ORVIA responsibility

ORVIA is responsible for:
- behaving according to the configured and approved workflow;
- preserving event and evidence provenance;
- reliable ownership, timers, notifications and acknowledgement tracking within the contracted service;
- making platform failures visible;
- maintaining the manual/continuity path;
- security, data isolation and records handling within the platform boundary;
- change control, monitoring, incident response and recovery for the platform.

## Human authority rule

Every observation can be preserved and heard. Decision rights still follow lawful professional and organisational authority.

## Emergency rule

No protective or statutory step may wait for an ORVIA acknowledgement, workflow gate, AI task, integration or service recovery. The action happens through the appropriate real-world route; ORVIA records and assures it afterwards.
